## 🔧 Phase 2: Project Mode (March 2026)

### 🎯 Goal: Portfolio mein 3-4 strong projects hone chahiye jo Ather ke kaam se related ho

### Project 1: Battery Pack Enclosure Design (Week 1-2)

**Kya karna hai:**
- [ ] Li-ion battery pack ke liye ek enclosure design karo SolidWorks/CATIA mein
- [ ] IP67 rating ko dhyan mein rakhte hue sealing mechanism design karo
- [ ] Thermal simulation karo ANSYS mein — heat dissipation check karo
- [ ] Vibration analysis karo — road conditions ke liye
- [ ] Manufacturing drawings banao — proper GD&T ke saath
- [ ] BOM (Bill of Materials) prepare karo
- [ ] Weight optimization karo — topology optimization try karo

**Kyun important:** Ather ka core product battery-powered hai. Battery enclosure design directly relevant hai.

### Project 2: Chassis/Frame Structural Analysis (Week 2-3)

**Kya karna hai:**
- [ ] Ek simple tubular/monocoque frame design karo electric two-wheeler ke liye
- [ ] Static structural analysis — rider weight + battery weight ke saath
- [ ] Fatigue analysis karo — potholes aur speed breakers simulate karo
- [ ] Material comparison study: Steel vs Aluminum vs Composite
- [ ] Weight vs Strength optimization karo
- [ ] FOS (Factor of Safety) calculations clearly present karo

**Kyun important:** Vehicle structure design Ather mein major work area hai.

### Project 3: Thermal Management System Design (Week 3-4)

**Kya karna hai:**
- [ ] Battery thermal management system ka concept design karo
- [ ] Liquid cooling loop design — pump, heat exchanger, coolant selection
- [ ] CFD simulation karo — ANSYS Fluent ya OpenFOAM mein
- [ ] Temperature distribution analysis under different operating conditions
- [ ] Ek simple prototype ya 3D printed model banana try karo

**Kyun important:** EV mein thermal management = safety + performance + battery life. Ather isko bahut seriously leta hai.

### Project 4: Personal/College Project (Ongoing)

**Kya karna hai:**
- [ ] Agar SAE/BAJA/Formula Student team mein ho toh us experience ko document karo
- [ ] Agar koi workshop/lab project kiya hai toh usko polish karke portfolio mein daalo
- [ ] GitHub pe project files upload karo (CAD files, reports, simulations)

## 💪 Phase 4: Interview Crack (Mid-Late April 2026)

### 🎯 Goal: Technical + HR interview mein full confidence se perform karna

### Technical Interview Prep

#### Core Mechanical Subjects (Yeh zaroor aayenge)

**Strength of Materials:**
- [ ] Stress-strain diagrams — mild steel, aluminum, polymer
- [ ] Bending moment aur shear force diagrams banana aana chahiye
- [ ] Torsion of shafts
- [ ] Beam deflection formulas
- [ ] Failure theories — Von Mises, Tresca, Max Normal Stress
- [ ] Fatigue failure — S-N curve, endurance limit

**Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer:**
- [ ] Laws of thermodynamics — statements + applications
- [ ] Heat transfer modes — Conduction, Convection, Radiation
- [ ] Fins ka concept — extended surface heat transfer
- [ ] Heat exchangers — LMTD method, NTU method
- [ ] Thermal conductivity of common materials

**Fluid Mechanics:**
- [ ] Bernoulli's equation aur uski applications
- [ ] Reynolds number — laminar vs turbulent flow
- [ ] Pressure drop in pipes
- [ ] Pump selection basics (centrifugal pump curves)
- [ ] CFD basics — meshing, boundary conditions, convergence

**Machine Design:**
- [ ] Bearing selection — ball vs roller vs journal
- [ ] Bolt/fastener design — preload, torque calculations
- [ ] Gear design basics — spur, helical
- [ ] Spring design — compression, extension
- [ ] Welded joint design

**Manufacturing:**
- [ ] Casting, Forging, Sheet Metal, Machining processes
- [ ] Tolerances — IT grades, surface finish
- [ ] Welding types — MIG, TIG, Spot welding
- [ ] CNC basics — G-codes, M-codes
- [ ] Quality control — SPC, Six Sigma basics

#### EV-Specific Questions (Ather ke liye special)

- [ ] "Battery thermal management system kaise design karoge?" — Prepared answer ready rakho
- [ ] "BLDC motor aur PMSM mein kya fark hai?" — Field orientation control bhi mention karo
- [ ] "IP67 rating kaise achieve karte hain enclosure mein?" — Gasket design, O-rings, sealing
- [ ] "Vehicle range kaise badhayenge?" — Aerodynamics, weight reduction, motor efficiency
- [ ] "Battery pack mein thermal runaway kaise rokenge?" — BMS, cell spacing, thermal barriers
- [ ] "Regenerative braking explain karo" — Energy recovery, motor as generator
- [ ] "EV mein NVH (Noise, Vibration, Harshness) challenges kya hain?" — Motor whine, gear noise
- [ ] "Ather 450X ke baare mein kya jaante ho?" — Specs, features, technology deep-dive karo

#### Practical/Design Questions

- [ ] "Ek pen stand design karo" — DFM principles apply karo
- [ ] "Kisi component ka weight kaise reduce karoge?" — Topology optimization, material change, rib design
- [ ] "Assembly mein interference aa raha hai, kaise solve karoge?" — Tolerance analysis, clearance fits
- [ ] "Kisi product mein crack aa raha hai field mein, root cause kaise nikaloge?" — Fishbone diagram, FEA

### HR / Behavioral Interview Prep

- [ ] **"Tell me about yourself"** — 2 min ka crisp intro ready rakho
- [ ] **"Why Ather?"** — Genuine passion dikhao, specific examples do
  - "Mujhe Ather ka approach pasand hai — technology-first mindset, in-house R&D, aur manufacturing sab India mein. Ather 450X ka teardown dekha tha, usmein jo engineering quality hai woh industry-leading hai"
- [ ] **"Why should we hire you?"** — Projects + skills + passion combo present karo
- [ ] **"Biggest challenge faced?"** — College project ya competition se example do
- [ ] **"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"** — EV industry mein contribute karna, product design leadership
- [ ] **"Strengths & Weaknesses?"** — Honest raho, weakness ke saath improvement plan bhi batao
- [ ] **"Any questions for us?"** — Zaroor poochho:
  - "Interns ko typically konse projects milte hain?"
  - "Ather mein engineering team ka day-to-day kaam kaisa hota hai?"
  - "New product development process kaisa hai Ather mein?"

### Mock Interview Practice

- [ ] 3-4 mock interviews do — friends ya seniors ke saath
- [ ] Camera on karke practice karo — body language check karo
- [ ] Apne projects ke baare mein 5 min fluently bol paane chahiye
- [ ] Time yourself — answers 2-3 min se zyada na ho
- [ ] STAR method use karo: Situation, Task, Action, Result

## 🚀 Phase 5: Ather Mein Entry! (May–July 2026)

### 🎯 Goal: Internship mein itna perform karo ki PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) mil jaaye

### First Week — Onboarding

- [ ] HR process complete karo — documents, ID card, system setup
- [ ] Team se milo — manager, mentor, teammates
- [ ] Codebase/design database samjho — existing projects review karo
- [ ] Tools setup karo — CATIA, ANSYS, PLM software jo bhi use ho raha hai
- [ ] Company culture samjho — meetings, communication style, work hours
- [ ] Office tour karo — R&D lab, testing facility, manufacturing plant (agar access hai)

### First Month — Learn & Contribute

- [ ] Assigned project ko deeply samjho — background research karo
- [ ] Mentor se regular 1-on-1 meetings rakho — weekly at minimum
- [ ] Questions poochho — "stupid question" jaisi koi cheez nahi hoti
- [ ] Small wins dhundho — koi quick improvement ya analysis karo
- [ ] Documentation likhna shuru karo — future mein kaam aayega
- [ ] Daily work log maintain karo — kya kiya, kya seekha, kya blockers hain

### Second-Third Month — Deliver Impact

- [ ] Project deliverables time pe complete karo
- [ ] Extra mile jao — sirf assigned kaam nahi, suggestions bhi do
- [ ] Cross-functional teams ke saath kaam karo — manufacturing, testing, electronics
- [ ] Presentation ready karo — apne kaam ka summary mid-internship mein dikhao
- [ ] Manager se feedback lo — "Kya accha ja raha hai? Kahan improve karun?"
- [ ] Network karo — lunch pe doosre teams ke logon se baat karo

### Last Week — Wrap Up & PPO Push

- [ ] Final presentation banao — problem, approach, results, impact
- [ ] Handover document likhne mein madad karo
- [ ] Manager aur mentor se feedback session rakho
- [ ] PPO ke baare mein politely poochho — "I've really enjoyed working here, I'd love to continue"
- [ ] LinkedIn pe connections add karo sabke
- [ ] Thank you email bhejo team ko
- [ ] Portfolio update karo — Ather experience add karo (NDA ka dhyan rakhna!)
